# Background
We want to participate in Prior Labs' TabPFN-3.5 Hackathon.
The descriptions of the event are in: https://platform.priorlabs.ai/hackathon-3.5

# Objective
Use TabPFN-3.5 to predict the delay of Deutsch Bahn trains based on historical data and other relevant features.
One known open-source dataset is available at: https://huggingface.co/datasets/piebro/deutsche-bahn-data

A few things that must be done initially:
- Understand how TabPFN-3.5 works and how it differs from the previous version.
- Peek at a subset of the dataset to see if it is suitable and usable.
